# Architecture decision checklist

- Define the outcome, constraints, scale, compliance needs, and measurable quality attributes.
- Capture current topology, ownership, dependencies, persistence, external systems, and operational pain.
- Separate confirmed facts from assumptions and open questions.
- Define systems of record, transaction boundaries, identifiers, invariants, retention, and migrations.
- Model dependency failures, partial success, duplicates, ordering, overload, and recovery.
- Define timeouts, bounded retries, idempotency, dead-letter handling, reconciliation, and rollback.
- Specify logs, metrics, traces, correlation IDs, SLOs, alerts, runbooks, backups, and restore tests.
- Record the decision, drivers, alternatives, tradeoffs, consequences, migration, and reconsideration triggers.
